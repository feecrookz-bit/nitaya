# Since the meeting: what changed on 14 September 2026

The owner pack describes the site as it stood at the meeting. This is the separate list of everything added or changed afterwards, in the order it went live on the preview (https://feecrookz-bit.github.io/nitaya/, same password). Nothing here is in the pack's screenshots unless the pack was rebuilt; the pack's decisions list has been kept current.

## 1. More of the yard's photographs
- Nine more photographs found on the current store's listings and blog: six added to the Kandla Grey, Raj Green, Fossil Mint and Copper Slate galleries, nine added to Projects (now 23), each captioned by what is visible and where the store files it.
- Captions over photographs made readable in both themes (a measured check now runs on every line of text laid over a photo).

## 2. Ordering rebuilt around the warehouse sheet
- **Pack sizes come from the "Database Products" sheet**, not the listings: mixed sandstone 18.19 m² (16/16/16/12); Kandla Grey 900 × 600, 40 slabs; Black Limestone 22 mm, 38 slabs; Fossil Mint as the active 900 × 600 pack of 37; 600 × 900 porcelain 40 slabs; 16 mm 600 × 600 pallets 80 slabs in boxes of 2; indoor tiles and cladding by the box. Five store listings now disagree with the sheet and want correcting (in the decisions list).
- **Every product page opens with a unit table**: per m², single slab (or "loose slabs by area" for mixed packs), pack or pallet, with the old price struck.
- **Order by the pack or by the area.** Type an area and the site says exactly what leaves the yard.
- **Every pack splits.** Single-size packs by the slab; mixed patio packs made up in the pack's own four sizes. 55 m² of Autumn Brown is 3 packs + 2 loose slabs = 55.01 m², not 4 packs = 72.76 m². The loose slabs are listed by size (1 × 600 × 600, 1 × 290 × 290). The current store's FAQ says only porcelain and Kandla Grey 900 × 600 split; it needs correcting.
- **Allowance for cuts** is a tick box with 5/10/15/20% in the calculator, the builder and the product page's by-the-area mode, so the customer adds or removes it before adding to the bag. The calculator also takes several areas.
- The bag and checkout show packs and loose slabs with their coverage, and one quantity function drives all of them, so they cannot disagree.

## 3. Delivery and collection
- Product page, bag and checkout show **"Delivery from Thu 17 Sep"** (three working days out), free over £500 inside the M25, and collection from Mark Road with the hours and a map link.
- **A calendar at checkout** to choose the delivery day (working days from the earliest, up to sixty days out) or the collection day. Confirmed by phone; no slot fees invented.

## 4. Offers pop-up
- Once a week per visitor, after a pause or when the mouse leaves for the tab bar: "Up to 24% off, across 22 ranges", built from the store's real was/now prices. Never on the bag, checkout or builder; Escape closes it.

## 5. Cladding as six products
- The store lists cladding once with six colourway photographs and no names. It is now six products, named by colour (Buff Mix, Silver Quartz, Mint, Slate Green, Kandla Grey, Pale Grey). The sheet names four cladding lines; which photograph is which is a question for the yard.

## 6. The stone in 3D
- **See it in 3D** on every product page: one slab turned in the hand, or laid in its pattern with pointed joints (a wall for cladding), dry or wet. The face is the product's own photograph.
- The turning slab is also a slide in every product's photo gallery, with a "3D" badge on its thumbnail.
- 15 September: the 3D stone rebuilt to look like the stone sold. True thickness (a 22 mm slab is 22 mm, not the chunky block it was), neutral light with no tone mapping so the face shows the photograph's own colour (measured: within a few percent of the texture on every range checked), fine relief taken from the photograph itself, a soft studio environment so wet and polished stone reflect properly, and twelve textures re-cut to a single clean slab face where the store's photograph showed joints, neighbouring tiles or a border (Black Limestone, Autumn Brown, Fossil Mint, Bodo White, Crystal Gris, Himalayan White, HS Beige, Sinai Pearl mixed, Calacatta Blanco, both Statuarios, Eden Ash). Textures now 1280 px.
- Wet is simulated until the hosed photographs come in; the yard has them, we need the originals.

## 7. Essentials
- The fifteen warehouse lines the store never listed are on the site: Joint-Tec (Pitch Black, Granite Grey, Buff Sand), Por-Tec (Midnight Grey, Ivory, Dove, Storm), primer, Kandla Grey and Quartz Grey step treads, Terra Grey and Polar Ivory edging, black and silver granite setts, black cobbles. Packing per pallet and per box from the sheet.
- No price shown or invented: "Priced at the counter" with an **Ask for a price** button that puts it in the bag as a quote. Under every sandstone, limestone and porcelain product a **Finish the job** strip offers the matching jointing compound and primer.

## 10. Shop filters, search and WhatsApp

- The shop filters by colour (Blacks, Whites, Greens and multi, Browns and rust, Creams and buffs, Greys), size and finish, as chips above the grid with a count and a "Clear filters" link. The filters live in the address, so a filtered view can be sent to a customer.
- A search box in the header on wide screens and in the menu everywhere, feeding the shop's existing search.
- A floating WhatsApp button, bottom right, on every page except checkout, plus a "WhatsApp the yard" row in the menu. It opens the yard's own number.

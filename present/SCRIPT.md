## If they ask

- **"Why dark?"** It suits stone photography and reads well in a yard in sunlight. One tap switches it and the site remembers. Their choice.
- **"Will we lose our Google traffic?"** The blog moves across with redirects from every old address before the domain switches. Nothing is deleted; the old site stays up until they say so.
- **"Where are our reviews?"** The two on the current site are in. We need a Google Business or Trustpilot link for more.
- **"It isn't taking payments."** By design. WooCommerce stays the system of record; the checkout you saw has the same fields and hands over to it.
- **"Why does delivery read differently?"** The demo follows their own Delivery Terms page (3–5 working days, free over £500 inside the M25). The current listings say other things in places. Whatever they confirm is what goes in.
- **"Can they see it laid in their garden?"** Yes, three ways, none needed for go-live. It's in the pack as an option.
- **"How long to go live?"** Answer with the order of steps, not a date: copy confirmed, photos shot, WooCommerce connected, blog and legal pages moved, delivery rates in, domain switched.

## Don't

- Don't quote costs or timescales from the page; there are none on it. Keep numbers for the conversation.
- Don't promise the visualiser; it's an option.
- Don't forward the password by email chain.

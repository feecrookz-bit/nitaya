# 11. History

Sixty-two commits over five days, from the commit log.

## 10 September: from a page to a store

A single-page prototype with drawn swatches and no photographs. The same day: a React rebuild with the 3D sandstone hero; the yard's own photography fetched from its media library and graded with a repeatable pass; the full catalogue; a rebuild as a store with shop, product pages, bag and checkout, studio renders and the dark design; editions, the patio builder and stone families; guides with drawings; the social template generator; the GitHub Pages deploy.

## 11 September: motion and the gate

The cinematic hero, marquee, count-ups and reveals. The password gate: the single-file build encrypted behind an unlock page, made to hold until the site frame has loaded, and to refuse an open deploy without the secret. The wet/dry slider, the delivery estimate, lazy 3D, guide diagrams, smarter search, bag coverage.

## 12 September: the design upgrade

One type system, self-hosted fonts, the taller hero, banners, pairings, the sticky buy bar, a fuller About. Dark theme as a second token set with a switch. Trade, Projects and Offers. The original logo restored everywhere. Header fixes for phones: the drawer, logo aspect, tap targets, hero text on pale photographs. `OPTIONS.md` written.

## 13 September: every photograph, then the audit

Every photograph the store has, on the product pages, with the detail from the listings. The full-screen gallery. The stone at scale on home and shop. The launch audit: fonts, copy, business details, accessibility, behaviour, print, with the fixes. The owner pack with the current site beside the new one, and the presenter script.

## 14 September: the meeting and after

The seven customer photographs from About and nine more from listings and the blog. Captions over photographs made readable. Then the ordering rework: by the pack or by the area, loose slabs, the unit table, delivery dates, the offer pop-up, pack sizes from the warehouse database. See it in 3D on every product; cladding as six colourways; the 3D slide in the gallery. Pack sizes settled by the sheet. The handover. The allowance for cuts as a tick box. Essentials. The checkout calendar. Every pack splits, mixed packs in their four sizes. `UPDATES.md`. Shop filters, header search, the WhatsApp button. The stock tracker brief for Coleisha and Simar. Hosting and ownership in the handover. The Cloudflare preparation: build script, redirects, headers, path shim, `DEPLOY.md`. The go-live audit and this wiki.
